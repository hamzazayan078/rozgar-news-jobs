<p>Payoneer remains the most widely supported payout platform across remote staffing platforms, freelance marketplaces, and international HR payroll tools. <h2>3. Digital Freelancer Accounts (Bank Alfalah, HBL, Meezan, UBL)</h2>
<p>Most major Pakistani banks now allow opening a dedicated <strong>Asaan Digital Freelancer Account</strong> or ESFCA (Exporters' Special Foreign Currency Account), letting you retain a portion of export proceeds in USD while converting the remainder at interbank rates rather than unfavorable grey-market spreads.</p>
