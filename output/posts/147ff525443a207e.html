Job Overview & Requirements

You know SaaS. You like helping people get it right. And you'd rather have a real conversation than a hard sell.

Do you speak German natively and want to help organisations get the most from a world-class SaaS platform? Then this might be the role you're looking for.

Join a lean-by-design, globally recognised B2B SaaS company as the German-speaking specialist for our DACH+ markets. No cold calling, no quotas — just meaningful conversations that help interested people discover how our software makes their work easier, then supporting them brilliantly once they're on board.

You won't be opening the market alone. Our marketing team already brings in German-speaking interest, and our experienced English-speaking sales colleagues handle these leads well. Your edge is simple and valuable: when a German-speaking prospect wants a demo auf Deutsch, or a new client wants onboarding and support in their own language, you're the person who makes that feel effortless — gründlich, verlässlich und auf den Punkt.

 

Join our Rebel Alliance

We're a diverse, remote crew that takes the work seriously without taking ourselves too seriously. You'll be a valued member of our global Client Success team, well supported with the tools, knowledge and people you need to do excellent work. In time you'll learn to work across teams and draw on our collective expertise — and you'll have real backing at every turn to grow into a confident product expert.

 

What you'll do

  • Run German-language demos for warm, interested leads — clearly and confidently

  • Become a product expert (with our support) and answer client and prospect questions accurately

  • Onboard and support clients in German and/or English, setting them up for success from day one

  • Provide responsive, thoughtful support in either German or English

  • Test scenarios carefully so your answers are technically correct, not guessed

  • Report recurring issues to help us improve the product

  • Build trust through honesty, empathy and reliability

 

What you bring (essentials)

  • Native-level German (C2) and strong business English

  • Minimum 3 years SaaS experience in support, onboarding, implementation or technical pre-sales

  • Real technical aptitude. Terms like HTML, CSS, JS, API, integrations, staging vs production, dev console and database queries are familiar — you needn't be a developer, but you should be genuinely comfortable around them and quick to learn the product's technical side

  • A confident communicator, written and spoken, who listens well

  • Comfortable discussing product value and guiding decisions — without the hard sell

  • Highly organised and process-driven; at home in a remote, tool-rich setup

  • Based in EMEA, ideally within DACH or close business-hours overlap (UTC+1/+2)

  • Reliable internet and a dedicated, distraction-free workspace

 

Nice to have

  • Familiarity with awards, grants, recognition or programme-management workflows

  • Experience supporting clients across both German and English

If you're technically sharp, genuinely enjoy helping people, and want to be the German-speaking specialist our DACH+ clients rely on, we'd love to hear from you. Komm an Bord!
